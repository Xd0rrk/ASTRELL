### Content Structure & Headings
**1. Unboxing as Physical UX Design**
- Why packaging is the first physical touchpoint of a brand promise.
- Framing the unboxing ritual as a deliberately choreographed user experience (UX) rather than just "getting the product out."

**2. The Engineering of Rigid Boxes**
- Moving away from collapsible folding cartons.
- The use of high-density chipboard (2mm-3mm) and precise V-cut scoring to achieve sharp, 90-degree architectural edges that signal premium quality.

**3. Friction and Tolerance Control**
- The psychology of resistance.
- Engineering the perfect "telescopic lid" fit: too loose feels cheap, too tight frustrates the user. Achieving the slow, hydraulic-like glide of a perfectly toleranced luxury box.

**4. The "Slow Reveal" Mechanics**
- Pacing the user journey through structural layers.
- Using magnetic snap closures (auditory feedback), ribbon pulls, and custom-molded EVA foam or velvet-wrapped inserts to cradle the product and delay instant gratification.

**5. Material Tactility and Finishing**
- Engaging the sense of touch.
- Wrapping structural forms in specialty uncoated papers, linen textures, or soft-touch laminates, proving that structural design and material science are inseparable in luxury packaging.
