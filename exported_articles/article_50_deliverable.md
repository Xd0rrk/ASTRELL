### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The Philosophy of High-End Retouching**
- In commercial photography, post-production is where technical rigor meets artistic intent.
- The philosophy of modern high-end retouching is "natural authenticity." The goal is not to create a plastic, flawless illusion, but to remove distractions while retaining the subject's unique character, skin texture, and material reality.

**2. The Order of Operations**
- A professional workflow is non-destructive and highly sequential.
- Phase 1: Ingest, cull, and Raw conversion (setting base exposure and white balance).
- Phase 2: Retouching (cleaning and sculpting).
- Phase 3: Color grading (establishing the final mood). Grading must always happen *after* retouching is complete.

**3. Frequency Separation: Preserving Texture**
- The industry standard for beauty, fashion, and portraiture.
- It involves mathematically dividing the image into a "High Frequency" layer (texture, pores, fine lines) and a "Low Frequency" layer (color and tone). This allows retouchers to smooth skin tones without destroying the essential, realistic texture of the skin.

**4. Dodge & Burn: Sculpting with Light**
- While automated plugins are common in consumer photography, high-end commercial work relies on manual Dodge & Burn.
- This time-intensive technique involves meticulously painting in highlights (dodge) and shadows (burn) at a micro-level to refine light, contour features, and subtly correct imperfections without blurring the image.

**5. Color Grading for Brand Consistency**
- Once the image is perfectly clean, the color grade is applied. 
- In commercial series, the grade must be uniform across dozens of images to maintain brand identity. We use advanced HSL adjustments, curves, and selective color tools to ensure the final output harmonizes perfectly with the client’s established brand tokens.
