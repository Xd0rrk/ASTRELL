### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The "Shadow AI" Brand Risk**
- Why employees using personal ChatGPT accounts to write emails and copy is destroying your brand consistency.
- The shift from ad-hoc prompting to centralized, persistent AI agents (Custom GPTs and Claude Projects).

**2. Building the AI Brand "Brain"**
- How to format a `brand_guidelines.md` document specifically for ingestion by an LLM.
- Moving from abstract adjectives ("be professional") to concrete rules ("use AP style, avoid exclamation marks").

**3. The Power of "Show, Don't Tell"**
- Why LLMs struggle with abstract descriptions of tone.
- Uploading 10-20 "Golden Examples" of high-performing, on-brand content (blogs, emails, ad copy) into your Claude Project/GPT knowledge base for the AI to emulate.

**4. Negative Constraints & Guardrails**
- The importance of telling the AI what *not* to do.
- Creating a "forbidden vocabulary" list to prevent AI buzzwords (e.g., "delve," "game-changer," "tapestry").

**5. Enterprise AI Governance**
- Establishing a "No-Shadow-AI" policy.
- Setting up approval workflows (Human-in-the-Loop) and categorizing AI usage by risk level (internal experiments vs. customer-facing copy).
