### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The D2C Shipping Challenge**
- Protecting products while delivering unboxing delight. The harsh reality of logistics networks versus the consumer's expectation of pristine luxury upon arrival.

**2. Right-Sizing and DIM Weight Optimization**
- Why standard box sizes kill profit margins. The financial impact of Dimensional Weight (DIM) pricing, and how custom-fit "right-sized" boxes reduce shipping costs while preventing product movement.

**3. Corrugated Engineering and Crush Resistance**
- Choosing the right flute (E-flute vs. B-flute). Engineering double-walled mailers that can withstand 10+ transit handling touchpoints without crushing structural corners.

**4. The "Trojan Horse" Design Strategy**
- Balancing security with branding. Why high-value D2C goods require a discreet, unbranded exterior to prevent porch piracy, while utilizing a vibrant, fully branded interior lid for the unboxing reveal.

**5. Strategic Void Fill and Inserts**
- Moving away from cheap bubble wrap. Designing custom die-cut corrugated inserts or using sustainable honeycomb paper to lock products in place, creating a premium presentation that doubles as shock absorption.
