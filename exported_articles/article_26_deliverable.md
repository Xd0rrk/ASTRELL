### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The End of the Static Brand Book**
- Why a 50-page PDF brand guidelines document is no longer sufficient for a global enterprise.
- The shift to living, centralized graphic design systems.

**2. The Architecture of Visual Tokens**
- Defining the smallest units of design: color codes, typography scales, and spacing units.
- Synchronizing digital tokens (RGB/HEX) with physical print standards (CMYK/Pantone) to prevent "brand drift."

**3. The Mathematics of Grid Systems**
- Grids are the invisible scaffolding of brand cohesion.
- Implementing an 8pt modular grid for digital and adapting it to physical aspect ratios (A4, Letter, Billboard).

**4. Global Consistency vs. Local Flexibility**
- The challenge of maintaining a unified brand while allowing regional teams to adapt to cultural nuances and varying text lengths (localization).
- Creating rigid core components alongside flexible "template hooks."

**5. Governance and System Maintenance**
- A graphic design system is a product, not a project.
- Establishing review cycles, semantic versioning, and a centralized portal to ensure global adoption.
