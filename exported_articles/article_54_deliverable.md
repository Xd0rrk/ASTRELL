### Content Structure & Headings
**1. Curing "Prompt Chaos" in Marketing**
- When every marketer on your team writes their own ad-hoc ChatGPT prompts from scratch, the result is fragmented brand voice and wasted hours.
- A centralized AI Prompt Library functions as a shared operational brain. It eliminates "prompt chaos," ensures brand consistency, and stops your team from reinventing the wheel for repetitive tasks.

**2. The Architecture of a "Prompt Card"**
- A successful library is highly structured. Every entry should function as a standardized "Prompt Card" with specific metadata.
- Essential fields: Descriptive Title, Category (SEO, Social, Email), Target AI Model (Claude vs. ChatGPT), and the exact Prompt Text using `[bracketed placeholders]` for easy variables. Crucially, include a "Gold Standard Output" example so the team knows what success looks like.

**3. Choosing Your Stack: Notion vs. Airtable**
- The tool you choose dictates how your team interacts with the system.
- **Notion** is ideal for highly visual, document-centric teams. It excels at centralizing "how-to" guides alongside the prompts. 
- **Airtable** is superior for data-driven operations. It allows you to track "Quality Scores," version history, and usage frequency via a relational database, helping you identify which prompts actually drive ROI.

**4. The CLEAR Framework for Templates**
- When stocking your library with its first 10 templates, use the CLEAR framework to ensure high-quality outputs.
- **C**ontext (Define the AI role), **L**ength (Specify word count), **E**xamples (Provide brand voice reference data), **A**udience (Define the reader), and **R**ules (List strict negative constraints).

**5. Maintenance: The Living System**
- A prompt library rots quickly if it isn't maintained. AI models update frequently (e.g., GPT-4 to GPT-4o), altering output styles.
- Implement version control and a peer-review feedback loop. If a marketer finds a prompt produces generic results, they must be able to log feedback and suggest tweaks. At ASTRELL, delivering a dynamic prompt library is a core component of our enterprise brand handover process.
