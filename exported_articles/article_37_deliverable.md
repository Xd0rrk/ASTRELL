### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The End of the Static Product Page**
- The limitations of traditional e-commerce grids. Why static JPEGs fail to bridge the physical-digital divide, leaving customers uncertain about texture, scale, and finish.

**2. CGI: Replacing the Traditional Photoshoot**
- The logistical nightmare of physical photoshoots (shipping prototypes, hiring studios, lighting inconsistencies).
- How photorealistic CGI (Computer-Generated Imagery) allows brands to generate perfect studio lighting, infinite angles, and colorways before the physical product even rolls off the manufacturing line.

**3. WebGL and 360° Interactive Showcases**
- Moving from passive viewing to active interaction.
- Utilizing WebGL and frameworks like Three.js to embed lightweight, 60fps 3D models directly into the browser. Allowing users to rotate, zoom, and inspect products in real-time without requiring app downloads or plugins.

**4. Real-Time Configurators & Personalization**
- Empowering the consumer. How 3D configurators allow buyers to instantly swap materials, colors, and components (e.g., configuring a luxury watch or a custom sofa) and see the results instantly, drastically reducing purchase hesitation.

**5. The ROI: Higher Conversions, Fewer Returns**
- The business case for 3D architecture. Data shows that interactive 3D visualization increases time-on-site, boosts checkout conversion rates, and critically, lowers return rates because the customer knows exactly what they are buying.
