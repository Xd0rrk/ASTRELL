### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The Digital Handshake**
- In a digital-first world, potential clients form an impression of a company’s reliability in milliseconds. 
- Professional corporate headshots serve as the "digital handshake," establishing credibility and reinforcing brand authenticity before a formal introduction ever takes place.

**2. Humanizing the B2B Relationship**
- B2B buyers face high-stakes, long-term decisions. They aren't just buying software or services; they are buying the expertise of the people delivering them.
- Seeing real, approachable faces behind a company name lowers psychological friction and moves the brand from a faceless entity to a partnership with relatable experts.

**3. Avoiding the "Stock Photo Tax"**
- Relying on generic stock images of people in suits shaking hands comes with a hidden cost: The Stock Photo Tax.
- It makes your brand look interchangeable with competitors and signals a lack of transparency. Authentic, custom team photography differentiates your brand and communicates your unique culture.

**4. The Measurable ROI of Team Imagery**
- While "trust" feels qualitative, the business impact is highly measurable.
- Increased Engagement: Consistent, high-quality team pages increase "dwell time" on your website. 
- Social Selling: Executive profiles on LinkedIn with professional photos are significantly more likely to receive messages, directly impacting outbound sales efforts.

**5. Engineering Visual Cohesion**
- A collection of mismatched selfies and outdated photos makes a company feel fragmented. 
- ASTRELL approaches team photography with the same rigor as product photography: ensuring consistent lighting ratios, unified background styling, and a color grade that perfectly matches your brand tokens.
