### Content Structure & Headings
**1. Beyond Responsive Design**
- Why simply using CSS media queries to stack desktop columns is not "mobile-first."
- The shift from mouse cursor precision to thumb ergonomics.

**2. The Biomechanics of the "Thumb Zone"**
- Mapping the mobile screen: Natural (Green), Stretch (Yellow), and Hard-to-Reach (Red) zones.
- Why critical CTAs and navigation must live in the bottom third of the screen.

**3. Applying Fitts's Law to Tap Targets**
- The mathematics of interaction: time required to move to a target.
- Enforcing the 48x48px minimum target size with 8px spacing to prevent "fat-finger" errors.

**4. Gestures vs. Buttons**
- Swipes, long-presses, and double-taps.
- The rule of visual affordance: gestures should enhance, not replace, visible controls.

**5. Designing for Distraction**
- The reality of mobile context: one-handed use, walking, partial attention.
- Why brutalist simplicity and high-contrast UI win in mobile environments.
