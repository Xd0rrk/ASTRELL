- Why subjective design reviews fail to improve conversion.
- Treating UX as a measurable, controllable business lever.

**2. Step 1: Quantitative Telemetry Analysis (The "Where")**
- Using GA4 and funnel tracking to identify drop-off points.
- Validating the specific screens that are bleeding revenue.

**3. Step 2: Heuristic Evaluation (The "Why")**
- Applying Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics.
- Evaluating for system feedback, error prevention, and cognitive load.

**4. Step 3: Behavioral Data & Session Recording (The "How")**
- Analyzing heatmaps and identifying "rage-clicks" or dead zones.
- Observing user hesitation to confirm quantitative findings.

**5. Step 4: The Prioritized UX Roadmap**
- Mapping findings on an Impact vs. Effort matrix.
- Transforming the audit from a list of complaints into a strategic backlog for engineering.
