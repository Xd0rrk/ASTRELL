### Content Structure & Headings
**1. Prototyping as Risk Mitigation**
- Why jumping straight into code is the most expensive way to build software.
- The role of prototyping in validating assumptions early.

**2. Low-Fidelity (Lo-Fi): Structuring the Skeleton**
- Using grayscale wireframes to focus on Information Architecture and user flows.
- Why lo-fi prevents stakeholders from getting distracted by colors and fonts.

**3. High-Fidelity (Hi-Fi): The "Fake" Software**
- Applying the design system (styles, typography, variants) to the skeleton.
- Why a hi-fi prototype should feel indistinguishable from the final coded product.

**4. Interactive Figma Prototyping**
- Using Smart Animate and interactive components to simulate real UI states (hovers, loaders, modals).
- Organizing prototypes into distinct "Flows" for usability testing.

**5. The Testing & Handoff Workflow**
- Conducting moderated vs. unmoderated usability tests.
- Handoff: Using the validated prototype as a flawless blueprint for the engineering team.
