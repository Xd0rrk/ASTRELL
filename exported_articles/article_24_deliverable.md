### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The Data Density Problem**
- The challenge of displaying 30+ KPIs to an executive without causing cognitive overload.
- Why traditional equal-width card layouts fail.

**2. The Bento Grid Layout**
- Defining the Bento UI pattern: deliberate asymmetry.
- Using spatial weight to signal importance (e.g., a 6-column hero tile for Revenue vs. a 1-column tile for System Status).

**3. Visual Hierarchy and Gestalt Principles**
- Guiding the user's eye naturally through layout rather than relying on heavy borders and text labels.
- Establishing a predictable anatomy for every tile (Header, Primary Data, Context).

**4. The "Drill-Down" Strategy (Progressive Disclosure)**
- Keeping the main dashboard as a high-level executive overview.
- Using linked actions and contextual tooltips to allow users to dive deep into the data without leaving the page.

**5. Responsive Analytics**
- How Bento grids gracefully degrade on mobile devices.
- Reordering tiles based on priority rather than simply shrinking complex charts.
