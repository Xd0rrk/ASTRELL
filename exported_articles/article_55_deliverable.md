### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The 80/20 Rule of Modern Post-Production**
- The introduction of AI tools like Photoshop's Generative Fill and Lightroom's AI masking hasn't replaced the commercial retoucher; it has elevated them from a pixel-pusher to an Editor-in-Chief.
- The most successful commercial workflows adopt an 80/20 hybrid approach: AI handles the 80% volume of tedious technical work, allowing the human artist to focus entirely on the 20% that requires subjective, creative judgment.

**2. What to Automate: The Baseline Workflow**
- Automation excels at high-volume, rule-based tasks where speed and consistency are paramount.
- **Culling & Sorting:** AI tools can instantly filter out blinks and blurs, reducing hours of manual sorting.
- **Batch Color & Exposure:** Normalizing exposure across thousands of images from a dynamic shoot location.
- **Repetitive Cleanup:** Background masking, automated dust/scratch removal, and formatting outputs for multiple social/print channels.

**3. What to Keep Human: The Creative Workflow**
- Human intervention remains non-negotiable for tasks that require context, emotional intelligence, and premium brand alignment.
- **High-End Skin Sculpting:** AI smoothing often results in a "plastic" look. Professional retouching relies on human-driven frequency separation and manual dodge & burn to retain natural pore texture.
- **Narrative Intent:** Only a human can grasp the specific narrative intent of a campaign, deciding if a shadow adds necessary dramatic depth or if it's merely a distraction.
- **Final Quality Control (QC):** Machines hallucinate. Human review is essential to catch artifacts, weird seams, or off-brand color shifts before final delivery.

**4. Strategic Integration: Lightroom to Photoshop**
- The modern industry-standard workflow relies on Lightroom as the automated "hub" and Photoshop for specialized intervention.
- Perform all global adjustments and batch corrections in Lightroom. Move to Photoshop only for localized, complex tasks (like using Generative Fill to extend a background for a specific web banner aspect ratio).

**5. Protecting the Brand Aesthetic**
- Efficiency should never come at the cost of brand integrity. 
- At ASTRELL, our post-production pipeline embraces AI to radically speed up delivery timelines, but every single image that leaves our studio passes through the critical eye of a human Art Director, ensuring "natural authenticity" is always preserved.
