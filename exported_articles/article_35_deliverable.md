### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The 3-Second Retail Battleground**
- Why FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods) packaging cannot rely on subtle, slow-burn aesthetics. The necessity of instant recognition in environments where 95% of purchase decisions happen on "autopilot."

**2. Visual Hierarchy and "The Read"**
- Structuring information architecture on a label. 
- The strict sequence of communication: 1. Brand Recognition (Color/Shape), 2. Product Variant (Flavor/Type), 3. Key Benefit (Organic/Sugar-Free). Eliminating visual chaos to guide the consumer's eye.

**3. The Strategy of Brand Blocking**
- Designing for the macro, not just the micro.
- How agencies design packaging to work in unison when faced together on a supermarket shelf. Creating a literal "block" of proprietary brand color that dominates the category space and disrupts competitor visibility.

**4. Behavioral Design and System 1 Thinking**
- Tapping into psychological triggers. Using distinctive brand assets (custom typography, proprietary structural shapes) to bypass rational evaluation and trigger habitual, emotional purchasing.

**5. Omnichannel Architecture**
- Why modern FMCG packaging must perform a dual role: it must stand out under harsh supermarket fluorescent lighting, but also remain instantly readable when scaled down to a 150px thumbnail on a digital grocery delivery app.
