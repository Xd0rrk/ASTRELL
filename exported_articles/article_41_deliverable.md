### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The Death of the "Blue Link"**
- The paradigm shift from traditional SERPs (Search Engine Results Pages) to AI-generated answers. Why brands can no longer rely on keyword stuffing to rank in the top 10 links.

**2. Defining AEO vs. GEO**
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** Structuring content to be the definitive, concise answer extracted by voice assistants and AI snippets.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** Building topical authority so Large Language Models (LLMs) trust, cite, and recommend your brand during complex query synthesis.

**3. Engineering Content for RAG Models**
- How AI search engines use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to search the live web.
- Why semantic HTML (strict H2/H3 hierarchies, bulleted lists) is critical. If an AI crawler cannot instantly parse the structure of your page, it will not synthesize your data into its answer.

**4. The Role of Structured Data (Schema Markup)**
- Providing explicit context to AI crawlers. How implementing advanced Schema.org markup (Organization, FAQ, Article) acts as a machine-readable map, ensuring AI models understand exactly what your content is.

**5. Building Topical Authority and Brand Co-occurrence**
- AI models learn associations. Why your brand must be mentioned alongside specific industry topics across high-authority external sites to build trust.
- Moving from isolated blog posts to comprehensive "Pillar and Cluster" content hubs that signal definitive expertise to the algorithm.
