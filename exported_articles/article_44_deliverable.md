### Content Structure & Headings
**1. The End of "Blue Link" Analytics**
- Why traditional SEO metrics (Rankings, Click-Through Rates, Sessions) are failing.
- AI Search Engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity) operate as "Zero-Click" environments. The user gets their answer directly from the AI, meaning your brand gains massive authority and visibility, but your Google Analytics traffic remains completely flat.

**2. The New KPIs of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**
- Shifting to rate-based tracking.
- **Brand Presence (Mention Rate):** The percentage of relevant, category-specific prompts where your brand is explicitly mentioned in the generated answer.
- **Citation Share:** Specifically for RAG-based engines like Perplexity, measuring how often your URL is used as a cited source.
- **Sentiment Score:** Qualitative tracking. Are you framed as a "leader," a "budget alternative," or a "legacy player"?

**3. Building an AI Measurement Framework**
- Creating Prompt Clusters: You cannot track a single keyword. You must track intent clusters (e.g., Awareness: "Best enterprise CRM", Comparison: "Salesforce vs Hubspot").
- The importance of continuous sampling: AI outputs are non-deterministic. A single manual check is noise; weekly tracking establishes a baseline trend.

**4. Tracking the "Silent" Influence**
- How to prove AI visibility is driving revenue when direct tracking links don't exist.
- Analyzing proxy metrics: Correlating spikes in your AI Visibility Score with subsequent increases in Branded Search Volume on traditional engines and un-attributed Direct Traffic in your analytics platform.

**5. Engineering Content for the AI Crawler**
- AI visibility doesn't happen by accident.
- To increase your Citation Share, your technical architecture must provide explicit semantic signals to AI bots (via strict H-tags and advanced Schema markup), proving your content is the most reliable, easily extractable data source on the web.
