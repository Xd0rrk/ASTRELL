### Content Structure & Headings
**1. Fixing It in Pre-Pro**
- The industry mantra. Why a successful commercial shoot is determined weeks before anyone picks up a camera.
- Pre-production is not just administrative work; it is risk management. It ensures that the client's budget is maximized on set, rather than wasted on solvable delays.

**2. Strategic Model Casting**
- Casting is more than finding attractive people; it is finding the physical embodiment of the brand persona.
- The process: Reviewing comp cards, conducting video castings, and managing talent usage rights (buyouts). Ensuring the cast accurately reflects the diversity and demographics of the target market.

**3. Location Scouting and the "Tech Scout"**
- A great location must be more than just aesthetically pleasing—it must be logistically viable.
- Evaluating natural lighting patterns, securing municipal permits, and ensuring adequate power supply. The importance of the "Tech Scout," where the art director and lighting tech walk the location to finalize equipment needs.

**4. Crewing and Logistics**
- Assembling the right team: Wardrobe stylists, hair and makeup artists (HMUA), digital techs, and lighting assistants.
- Coordinating equipment rentals and establishing a clear chain of command on set to ensure the creative director's vision is executed flawlessly.

**5. The Call Sheet: The Source of Truth**
- The most important document in production. Distributed 24 hours before the shoot.
- What it includes: Call times (staggered for crew vs. talent), exact location maps, parking instructions, contact information, the day's shot list, and safety protocols. It ensures a crew of 20+ people operates as a single, synchronized unit.
