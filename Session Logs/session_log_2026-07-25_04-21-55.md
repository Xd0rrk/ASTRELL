## Important Decisions & Reasoning

1. **Cardboard Texture Integration at Processing Time**:
   - *Reasoning*: Compositing paper grain directly into WebP textures via `sharp` ensures 0 runtime CPU/GPU overhead while producing photorealistic cardboard textures.

2. **Global Drag Window Event Listeners**:
   - *Reasoning*: Attaching `mousemove` and `mouseup` to `window` during drag prevents cursor loss when dragging quickly across the screen and eliminates accidental text selection over hero headers.

3. **WebP Compression Strategy**:
   - *Reasoning*: Converting 4.6K source images to 2K WebP with effort level 5 reduced asset payload from 72.8 MB to 780 KB without visible degradation in text or artwork quality.
