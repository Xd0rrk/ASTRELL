## Important Decisions & Reasoning

1. **Grid Padding for Sub-Categories**:
   - *Reasoning*: A 6-column grid requires at least 18 items to fill a 6×3 layout block. Filtering to smaller categories (e.g. 2 packaging items) left 80% of the grid blank, creating black voids and wrapping glitches. Repeating category items up to 18 items guarantees a 100% full grid without empty gaps.

2. **Clean Minimalist Hero Focus**:
   - *Reasoning*: Removing big textual overlay headings allows visitors to immediately engage with the visual creative work without distraction.

3. **Dynamic Dimension Tracking via `ResizeObserver`**:
   - *Reasoning*: Standard `getBoundingClientRect()` on mount uses fixed dimensions. Using `ResizeObserver` ensures wrap boundaries update dynamically when viewport or element sizes change.
