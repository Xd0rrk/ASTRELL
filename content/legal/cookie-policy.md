## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow a website to recognise your device and remember information about your visit, such as preferences and actions taken. Similar technologies include pixels, local storage, and tracking scripts, which we refer to collectively as "cookies" in this Policy.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies
These cookies are essential for our website to function properly (e.g. enabling secure browsing, load balancing, and basic site navigation). They are typically set by our hosting and security infrastructure providers, including **Vercel** and **Cloudflare**. These cookies cannot be switched off without affecting core site functionality.

### 3.2 Analytics Cookies
We use **Google Analytics** to understand how visitors use our website, including which pages are visited, how long visitors stay, and how they arrived at our site. This helps us improve our website and services. Analytics data is generally aggregated and used for statistical purposes.

### 3.3 Functional Cookies

### 3.4 Backend and Infrastructure Cookies

### 3.5 Third-Party Cookies
Some cookies are placed by third-party services embedded in our website (for example, analytics or embedded content providers). These third parties may use cookies in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

## 4. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

## 5. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

- **Browser settings:** Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions, as the process varies by browser and device.
- **Analytics opt-out:** You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking using tools such as the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
- **Cookie banner/consent tool:** Where we implement a cookie consent banner on our website, you may adjust your preferences there at any time.

Please note that disabling certain cookies, particularly strictly necessary cookies, may affect the functionality and performance of our website.
